# Entry Rules

What a contributor must do for an entry to count as valid in the Khmer Household Archive.

## 1. Name

| Rule | Valid | Invalid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khmer name is written in Khmer script | ត្បាល់ | `Tbal`, empty | Not Khmer script, or missing |
| English name is written in English | Stone mortar and pestle | `ត្បាល់`, empty | Not English, or missing |

## 2. Province

The province where your family uses the object.

| Rule | Valid | Invalid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Must be one of Cambodia's 25 provinces (list below) | Kampong Cham | `Bangkok`, `Ho Chi Minh City` | Not in Cambodia |
| Province level only, not district, village, or street | Phnom Penh | `Toul Kork`, `Street 271`, `Prek Kdam village` | Too specific, and a privacy risk |
| Not wider than a province | Siem Reap | `Cambodia`, `Asia`, `the countryside` | Too vague to mean anything |
| Use the province name, not a town inside it | Preah Sihanouk | `Sihanoukville`, `Kampong Som` | A town, not the province |
| One province per entry | Takeo | `Takeo and Kampot` | Pick the household the entry is about |
| Spell it exactly as in the list | Battambang | `Batdambang`, `battambong`, `BTB` | Breaks filtering and search |
| A real place, not a description | Kandal | `my house`, `grandma's home`, `N/A` | Not a province |

**Accepted provinces (25):**
Banteay Meanchey, Battambang, Kampong Cham, Kampong Chhnang, Kampong Speu, Kampong Thom, Kampot, Kandal, Kep, Koh Kong, Kratie, Mondulkiri, Oddar Meanchey, Pailin, Phnom Penh, Preah Sihanouk, Preah Vihear, Prey Veng, Pursat, Ratanakiri, Siem Reap, Stung Treng, Svay Rieng, Takeo, Tbong Khmum

## 3. Source and link

**Source** = who the knowledge came from. **Link** = an optional reference or photo.

| Rule | Valid | Invalid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source is required: the person in your family you learned it from, by relationship | Grandmother, My father, Aunt (mother's side) | empty, `Google`, `ChatGPT`, `common knowledge` | The archive is built on family knowledge, not searches |
| Source uses relationship, not full name or contact details | Grandmother | `Sok Chanthy, 012 345 678` | The site is public |
| If you saw it yourself, say so | Myself, seen at home | `me lol` | Be clear, not casual |
| Link is optional. If added, it starts with `https://` and is complete | `https://cambodiancookbook.com/equipment/` | `cambodiancookbook.com`, `www.site.com`, `C:\Users\me\photo.jpg` | Incomplete or local links do not open on the site |
| Link opens for anyone, without login | A public web page | A private Facebook post, a Google Drive file set to private | Readers cannot open it |
| Link goes to one specific page about the object | The page describing the tbal | `https://google.com/search?q=mortar`, a site homepage | Not a source, just a starting point |
| No shortened or AI chat links | The full page address | `bit.ly/3xYz`, a ChatGPT or Claude share link | Hides where it goes, or is not a real source |
| A photo link opens the image directly and shows the object | `https://…/krolor.jpg` of the rice tin | A link to a page with the photo on it, a stock photo, a photo of a dish | Will not display, or wrong subject |
| A link backs up the entry. It does not replace your source | Source: Grandmother. Link: cookbook page for spelling | Source empty, only a link | Family source comes first |

## 4. Description

| Rule | Valid | Invalid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Required, 2 to 5 full sentences | "The empty condensed-milk tin is our rice measure. It lives in the rice sack, and two cans feed the family." | `rice can`, `Used for cooking.` | Too short to tell anyone anything |
| Describes the object itself: what it is and what it does | "A heavy stone bowl we pound kroeung in." | "Amok is a steamed fish curry..." | Describes a dish, not the object |
| Says how YOUR household uses it: who, when, or where it is kept | "Grandmother keeps it by the stove and uses it every morning." | "A mortar is a tool used in many cultures to grind spices." | Generic definition, could be any country |
| Written in your own words | Written from what you saw or were told | A paragraph pasted from a website, book, or AI tool | Copied content, and it loses the family angle |
| Facts only, no guessing presented as fact | "Grandmother says it came from her mother." | "It is 500 years old." (with no source) | Unverifiable claim |
| No private info | "My aunt uses it." | Phone numbers, house addresses, full names | The site is public |
| Real text, not filler | Full sentences | `"   "`, `...`, `test`, `asdf` | Looks filled but says nothing |
| Khmer words are fine inside English sentences | "We pound kroeung in it." | (none) | Allowed |
